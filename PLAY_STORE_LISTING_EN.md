## Full description

Takip Analizi is a local-first utility for reviewing Instagram and X follow relationships from your official data archive files.

### Analyze without sharing passwords

You do not need to enter your Instagram or X password. The app does not sign in to your social-media accounts, does not use private APIs, and does not automate following or unfollowing.

### Analyze official archives on your device

• Import Instagram data-export ZIP, JSON, or HTML files.
• Select your official X data archive as a ZIP file.
• For very large X archives, select follower.js and following.js directly.
• X media, posts, and message history unrelated to follow analysis are not used for this purpose.

### See follow relationships in clear categories

• Not Following Back
• Mutual
• Follows You
• Unfollowers
• New Followers

### Compare changes over time

Analysis snapshots can be stored locally on your device. When you import a newer archive for the same account, Takip Analizi can automatically compare it with the previous snapshot. You can also manually compare two historical snapshots.

### Manage lists

Search by username, sort A–Z or Z–A, open profile links in an external Instagram/X app or browser, and hide accounts you do not want to see by adding them to the Ignore list.

### Reports and local data management

Copy an analysis report or save it as a TXT file. You can delete analysis history, ignored-account preferences, or all local app data from inside the app. These deletion actions do not change your actual Instagram or X account data.

### Privacy approach

Your follow-archive files are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. The current app does not require an internet connection for analysis and does not request the Android INTERNET permission. If you choose to open a social-media profile, the link is handed off to the relevant third-party app or browser.

Takip Analizi is an independent ZMila Studio application and is not developed, sponsored, endorsed, or approved by Instagram or X.
